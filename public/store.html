helpQuestions

Frequently asked questions

Is the first term really free?

Yes. Your school's first term costs nothing. You only start paying from the next term, based on your student and teacher numbers.

How do we pay?

We send an invoice each term in FCFA. You can pay by mobile money, bank transfer or cash. Schools that join after the first term pay a one-time 5,000 FCFA commitment fee.

How is the bill calculated?

We use the highest number of students and teachers you had during the term, multiplied by the price of your packages.

What happens if we pay late?

After a grace period, the school becomes read-only — you can still view everything but not make changes. In Term 3, exports and statistics are locked until the invoice is paid.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Meridian works in any web browser and as a phone app, so teachers can check in and enter marks from their phones.

Can we use both English and French?

Yes. Your workspace can be in English or French, with Anglophone and Francophone sections, and report cards in either language or both.
