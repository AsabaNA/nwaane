These terms are the agreement between Meridian (“we”) and the school that creates an account (“the school”). By creating an account or using the platform you accept them. Please read them together with our Privacy Policy.

1. The service

Meridian is an online platform for schools in Cameroon: marks and report cards, school fee records and receipts, timetables, student and teacher attendance, ID cards, exam papers and related tools, in English and French. The service is organised in three modules: A. Report cards and attendance, B. School fees and C. Teacher attendance. Each school chooses the modules it uses.

2. Accounts and responsibilities

  • The person who signs a school up confirms that they are authorised to do so for the school, and becomes its principal account.
  • The school is responsible for its users, for keeping passwords private, and for everything done under its accounts.
  • The school is responsible for the accuracy of the information it enters and for having the right to enter it, including informing staff and parents about how it uses the platform.
  • Receipts, report cards and statistics produced by the platform are based on what the school entered. The school remains responsible for its own accounting, grades and pay decisions.

3. Money: the platform never holds school fees

The platform records school fee payments that the school receives itself. We do not collect, hold or move school fee money, and we are not a party to any payment between a school and parents.

4. Prices and billing

  • Prices are in FCFA, per term: per student for modules A and B, and per teacher for module C. Combining modules lowers the price. The current prices are shown on our website and in the platform under My Plan.
  • The first term is free. A school that registers after the first term of the school year pays a one-time registration fee (currently 5,000 FCFA) before it can use the platform.
  • We invoice at the end of each term, based on the highest number of students and of teachers with lessons reached during that term, so the bill follows what the school actually used.
  • Price changes apply from the next term. We announce them in advance and they never change an invoice already issued.
  • Invoices are paid through the platform with MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money (via Fapshi), or by another method we agree with you. Payment fees charged by the payment provider are ours to bear, not the school’s.

5. Adding and removing modules

  • A school can add a module at any time. It is available immediately and is billed on the term invoice.
  • A module can be removed only when the school has no unpaid invoice. The removal takes effect at the end of the current term, and that term is still billed. The school’s data is kept, so adding the module again restores it.
  • A school must always keep at least one module.

6. Late payment

  • An invoice is due on the date shown on it. If it stays unpaid for 14 days after that date, the school’s account becomes read-only: users can view and print but cannot enter new information until the invoice is paid.
  • For Term 3, schools close for the holidays, so the Term 3 invoice is issued shortly before the term ends. Until it is paid in full, file downloads and statistics for that term are locked, while data entry continues.
  • As soon as the invoice is paid, access returns in full.

7. Refunds

Amounts paid for a term that has started are not refundable, except where the law requires it or where we made a billing error, which we will correct.

8. Acceptable use

You agree not to: break the law; enter information you have no right to enter; try to access another school’s data; attack, overload or reverse-engineer the service; share accounts outside your school; or use the platform to send spam or harmful content. We may suspend an account that does this.

9. Your data

The school owns its data. We use it only to provide the service, as described in our Privacy Policy. A school can download its data at any time, and can ask us to delete it when it leaves.

10. The AI assistant

The AI assistant is optional and off until the principal turns it on. It answers only with information the signed-in person is allowed to see. Its answers can be wrong, so check anything important before acting on it.

11. Availability and changes

We work to keep the platform available and backed up, but we cannot promise it will never be interrupted or free of errors. We may improve, change or remove features, and will tell schools beforehand when a change affects how they work.

12. Limits of our responsibility

To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for indirect or consequential losses, for losses caused by information entered incorrectly, by a school’s own decisions, or by problems outside our control such as power or network failures. Our total responsibility to a school for any claim is limited to the amount that school paid us in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing in these terms limits responsibility that the law does not allow us to limit.

13. Ending the agreement

A school can stop using Meridian at any time, after paying any invoice already issued. We may suspend or end an account for serious or repeated breaches of these terms, or for non-payment after notice. On ending, the school can download its data, and we handle deletion as described in the Privacy Policy.

14. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We will tell schools in the platform or by email before a material change takes effect. Continuing to use the platform after that means you accept the new terms.

15. Governing law

1. Le service

Meridian est une plateforme en ligne pour les écoles du Cameroun : notes et bulletins, suivi de la scolarité et reçus, emplois du temps, présence des élèves et des enseignants, cartes, sujets d’examen et outils associés, en français et en anglais. Le service est organisé en trois modules : A. Bulletins et présence, B. Scolarité et C. Présence des enseignants. Chaque école choisit les modules qu’elle utilise.

2. Comptes et responsabilités

  • La personne qui inscrit une école confirme qu’elle y est autorisée, et devient le compte principal (chef d’établissement) de l’école.

La plateforme enregistre les paiements de scolarité que l’école reçoit elle-même. Nous n’encaissons, ne détenons ni ne transférons l’argent de la scolarité, et nous ne sommes partie à aucun paiement entre une école et les parents.

4. Tarifs et facturation

  • Les prix sont en FCFA, par trimestre : par élève pour les modules A et B, et par enseignant pour le module C. Combiner des modules réduit le prix. Les tarifs actuels figurent sur notre site et dans la plateforme, sous Mon offre.
  • Le premier trimestre est gratuit. Une école qui s’inscrit après le premier trimestre de l’année scolaire paie des frais d’inscription uniques (actuellement 5 000 FCFA) avant de pouvoir utiliser la plateforme.
  • Nous facturons à la fin de chaque trimestre, selon le plus grand nombre d’élèves et d’enseignants ayant des cours atteint pendant ce trimestre : la facture suit donc l’usage réel de l’école.
  • Les changements de prix s’appliquent à partir du trimestre suivant. Nous les annonçons à l’avance et ils ne modifient jamais une facture déjà émise.
  • Les factures se paient dans la plateforme par MTN Mobile Money ou Orange Money (via Fapshi), ou par un autre moyen convenu avec vous. Les frais prélevés par le prestataire de paiement sont à notre charge, pas à celle de l’école.

5. Ajouter et retirer des modules

  • Un module ne peut être retiré que si l’école n’a aucune facture impayée. Le retrait prend effet à la fin du trimestre en cours, et ce trimestre reste facturé. Les données de l’école sont conservées : ajouter à nouveau le module les restaure.
  • Une école doit toujours conserver au moins un module.
